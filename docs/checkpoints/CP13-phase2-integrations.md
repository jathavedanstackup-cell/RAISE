# CP13 — POS/reservation integrations & multi-location (Phase 2, NOT v1)

Source: `docs/production-plan.md`, Part 6.

**Do not start this until v1 (CP0-CP12) is live with at least one real restaurant.** This is explicitly out of scope for the initial launch — see Part 1.6 (non-goals for v1) of `docs/production-plan.md`.

**Done when**: at least one real POS integration round-trips a visit; a chain owner can view aggregated visit/timing metrics across locations.
